SLIP

How to play: slide rows and columns until the four rows spell four hidden words, in any order. Drag a tile along its row or column, or tap an arrow. Letters that slide off one edge come back on the other. Nothing is marked until you press Check. Use all three checks without solving it and the game ends. Tap a 🔓 beside a row or column to lock it so it cannot move. Your result shows whether you used any locks.
